      Ranking is only half the story

      Logged ranking compares items already shown by the original news site. Catalog retrieval must first find clicked stories among all previously available articles. Its recall is much lower.

      Keep the limits in view

      Unknown exposure probabilities prevent causal claims. Session feedback timing is simulated. First observation is a freshness proxy. One training seed does not establish statistical significance.
